Approach

Diagnose before designing

Every project starts with surfacing misaligned assumptions. Shared vocabulary doesn’t mean shared meaning, and the reference interview trained me to find the gap between what people ask for and what the problem is. Case study 03 is that under a deadline: a six-month stall, resolved in five days.

Define the objects before the interface

Domain modeling, taxonomy design, and metadata standards come before wireframes. The structure is the decision.

Cross-functional by default

Embedded with dev, business, and governance stakeholders rather than handed off to them. Influence through clarity of craft, not organizational hierarchy.

Documentation that survives turnover

Systems designed so the logic is clear enough that people use the docs to train the next person.
